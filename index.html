What is Dessa AI?

Dessa AI is a full-duplex speech-to-speech voice model from Block — a Dessa voice trained only on human conversation. No text, no pretrained models: it learned to talk simply by listening, the way people do.

Because it starts with sound, Dessa hears what text alone can’t carry: tone, timing, pauses, laughter, and emotion. It listens and speaks at the same time, so you can interrupt, laugh, or add a quick “mm-hm” without waiting for a turn. Block trained it on a single NVIDIA GB200 NVL72, and says it runs locally on a MacBook Pro M4 Max or better. It is a research preview — and the 21 original demo recordings on this page let you hear it for yourself.
